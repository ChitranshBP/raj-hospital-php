## 2. Content Brief

### Topic Overview
Blog post about understanding haemoglobin in Hindi - what it is, normal levels, causes of low/high haemoglobin, symptoms, foods to increase it, and prevention tips.

### Target Audience
- Hindi-speaking individuals experiencing fatigue or weakness
- People with family history of anaemia or blood disorders
- Health-conscious people in Ranchi/Jharkhand region
- Women with heavy menstrual bleeding
- Patients with chronic diseases affecting blood

### Content Goals
- Explain haemoglobin in simple Hindi
- Cover normal levels for different groups
- List causes and symptoms of low/high haemoglobin
- Provide dietary recommendations
- Build trust for RAJ Hospital's diagnostic services
