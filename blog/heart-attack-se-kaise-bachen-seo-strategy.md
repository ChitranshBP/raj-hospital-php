# SEO Strategy: Heart Attack Se Kaise Bachen Blog Post

This document outlines the SEO strategy for the Hindi blog post on heart attack prevention published at `/blog/heart-attack-se-kaise-bachen/`. The page targets Hindi-speaking audiences in Ranchi and Jharkhand seeking heart health information.
