- **Primary Keyword**: asthma symptoms in hindi / दमा के लक्षण
- **Search Intent**: Informational - People searching for asthma symptoms in Hindi
- **Language**: Hindi (Targeting Hindi-speaking audience in India)
- **Primary Location**: Ranchi, Jharkhand, India
